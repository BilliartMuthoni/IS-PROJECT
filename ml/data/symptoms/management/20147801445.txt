Cultural Management of Late Blight of Potato (Nepal, September 2012)

• Use disease-free certified seed tubers, suggested by the agriculturist • Planting in February is desirable • Follow crop rotation in infested areas. Don't use susceptible crops like, tomato, brinjal and chilli. • Watch the crop regularly and if the disease is found, destroy the affected area by burning • Irrigate the field early in the morning so that the leaves do not remain wet for too long • Digging and ridging is necessary to prevent the disease attacking the stems • Remove all the aerial parts 2-3 weeks before harvesting. These can be used for composting. • After harvest, separate the diseased tubers then store the healthy tubers in a shaded and cool place • Don't wash the tubers before storage as this will spread the disease • Frequently monitor the tubers in storage and remove any infested ones
