Potato Late Blight (Pakistan, September 2013)

• Sort out or reject the infected tubers at the harvesting/storage stage • Disinfect agricultural tools: use bleach solution at 2g/litre of water • When there are low temperatures and high humidity, apply a preventive spray on the crop, e.g. mancozeb @ 2g /litre of water with 10-14 day intervals • Avoid too much irrigation during low temperature days • Cultivate resistant/tolerant varieties, e.g. FSD White, available at Punjab Seed Corporation • 2-3 sprays of (mancozeb+ metalaxyl @ 2g /litre of water) just after the appearance of disease to overcome further spread
