Late blight or barafu in tomatoes (Kenya, October 2012)

CULTURAL METHODS • Plant tomatoes 90cm by 30cm or 75cm by 45cm or 60cm by 60cm. • Wash tools with clean water after use to avoid spread of the disease. • Weed regularly and remove crops like potatoes and black nightshade. Remove diseased leaves and plant parts from the field. • Do not grow tomatoes every year in the same place: alternate with onion then beans and cabbage. • Keep two suckers per plant for tall varieties like money maker, Anna FI and star 9082 F1. • If you plant short varieties such as Riogrande and Kilele F1 cover the soil with dry grass or wheat straws. Do not use weeds since they could spread pests and diseases. CHEMICAL CONTROL Avoid spraying on windy days and spray at least 4 hours before it rains. Add sticker and spreader to the chemicals to enable them to stay on the plant. • During wet and cold weather, spray preventive fungicide such as mancozeb, copper-based products, etc. • In case of attack, spray fungicides containing metalaxyl or metalaxyl M in addition to mancozeb to halt the spread of the disease.
