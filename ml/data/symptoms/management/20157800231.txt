Late Blight of Potato (Bangladesh, September 2013)

• Use of resistant or tolerant varieties of potato viz. BARI Alu-46, BARI Alu-53, BARI-Alu-77, BARI Alu-90 and BARI Alu-91 • Cultivation of early variety • Use disease-free/clean seed for planting • Use spacing of 60 x 30 centimetres • When cool and foggy weather starts, preventive measures must be taken by spraying with fungicides, like mancozeb (e.g. Dithane M-45/Indofil M-45/ Haymancozeb) or iprovalicarb and propineb (e.g. Melody Duo) @ 0.2%. Spray weekly if the weather is wet and every two weeks if the weather is dry and cold until two weeks before harvest. Spray when the leaves are dry. • Inspect the field regularly for signs of blight • Irrigation must be stopped as soon as late blight infection is noticed • With the start of late blight infection, spray with metalaxyl and mancozeb, e.g. Ridomil Gold @ 35 ml per 20 litre knapsack sprayer, 2-3 times at 7 days interval. Alternatively, spray with one of the following: ◦ Fenamidone and mancozeb, e.g. Secure @ 2gm/L water ◦ Iprovalicarb and propineb, e.g. Melody Duo @ 2gm + fenamidone and mancozeb, e.g. Secure @ 1gm /L water ◦ Iprovalicarb and propineb e.g. Melody Duo @ 2gm + mancozeb e.g. Dithane M-45 @ 2gm / L water • Remove and destroy all stems and leaves at least two weeks before harvesting, so the disease does not spread from the leaves to the potatoes when harvesting.
