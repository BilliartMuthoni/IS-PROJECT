Bacterial leaf spot (Trinidad and Tobago, November 2011)

• Use disease-free seedlings. • Avoid overhead sprinklers. Use drip irrigation to avoid splashing soil on the plants. • Do not overcrowd the plants in the field. Leave some space between them. • Mulch can reduce the spread of the disease by reducing soil splash. Plastic mulch works well. • After harvest, do not plant sweet peppers, tomato or hot peppers in the same soil. • Use copper-based fungicides in combination with mancozeb at the recommended rates. Spray them every 7 days, or every 14 days if the disease is bad. • Follow the recommendations on the label for the safe period of application of these fungicides before harvesting. • Biological disease control products can also be used.
